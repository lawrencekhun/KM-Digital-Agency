Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

KM Ventures Group LLC, doing business as KM Digital Agency ("KM Digital," "we," "us"), provides websites, review requests, missed-call text-back, and lead response services to local businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers our website (kmdigitalagency.com) and the calls and text messages we handle for our client businesses.

1. Information we collect

From visitors to our website

  • Information you give us when you book a call or contact us, such as your name, business name, email address, and phone number.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends automatically, such as device type, browser, and pages visited.

From customers of the businesses we serve

When you call, text, or message a business that uses our services, we process information on that business's behalf, including:

  • Your phone number and the date and time of your call or message
  • Your name, address, and the details you share about the service you need
  • The content of text messages and website chat conversations
  • Appointment details if you book a time
  • Feedback you choose to share about your experience

2. How we use information

  • To reply to your call or message and pass your request to the business you contacted
  • To book appointments or estimates you request
  • To send review requests and service-related follow-ups on behalf of the business you used
  • To answer common questions through automated replies
  • To provide our clients with reports on calls, leads, and reviews
  • To operate, secure, and improve our services, and to meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

3. Text messaging (SMS)

How you opt in. You may receive a text message from a business we serve after you call that business and the call is not answered, after you submit an inquiry or request a quote, or after you receive a service from that business. Messages relate only to your inquiry, appointment, or service.

No sharing of mobile information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed.
  • Help: reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the details below.
  • Message frequency varies based on your interaction with the business.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. How information is shared

We share information only as needed to provide our services:

  • With the business you contacted, so they can respond to you and provide the service you asked about.
  • With service providers who help us operate, such as our telephone and messaging provider (Twilio), hosting and database providers, calendar providers, and AI providers that help generate replies to common questions. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law, to respond to legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of people or our business.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

As stated in Section 3, mobile phone numbers and text messaging opt-in data are never shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed to provide our services to the business you contacted, to produce their reports, and to meet legal requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, including encrypted connections and restricted access. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us below. If we hold your information on behalf of a business you contacted, we may refer your request to that business.
  • California residents may have additional rights under California law, including the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
